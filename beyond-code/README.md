# Beyond Code

Software development is a big part of my life. I also learn through volunteering, practical problem solving, and meeting other people. Here are four stories about those experiences.

## Stories

### [Growth Beyond Code: Fitness and Volunteering](0027_growth-beyond-code.md)
I share my fitness journey and my volunteer work with the Malteser in Frankfurt. At Café Digital, I help older people with questions about their devices. These experiences have taught me consistency, patience, and communication.

### [Repairing My Dell Laptop's Charging Port](dell-inspiron-7610-dc-jack-reparatur-2026.md)
I found the cause of a charging problem on my Dell Inspiron 16 Plus 7610 and replaced its DC jack myself. The article covers my troubleshooting steps, the repair, the costs, and what I learned.

### [A Day with the Malteser in Fulda](sternwallfahrt-malteser-fulda-2026.md)
I joined a small Malteser group from Frankfurt for a pilgrimage to Fulda in October 2026. I write about the people I met, our visit to Fulda Cathedral, and the value of community.

### [IBM Bob Coding Watch Party in Frankfurt](ibm-bob-coding-watch-party-frankfurt-2026.md)
I attended a developer event about IBM Bob, AI agents, and modernizing older software. I share what I learned from live demos and conversations with other developers.
